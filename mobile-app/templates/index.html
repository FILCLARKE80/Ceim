About

Residential Property Price Index

This app provides an interactive way to explore residential property price indices across different regions and property types.


Base Year: 2015 (Index = 100)

Property Types: Detached, Semi-Detached, Terraced, Flat

Data Range: 2020 - 2025


An index value above 100 means prices have increased relative to the base year. For example, an index of 140 means prices are 40% higher than in 2015.
